## Avalor (CotAS setting)

- Floating city, kept aloft by leyline currents and the **Phoenix Heart** buried beneath it
- Pre-Cataclysm name: **Ya'valhur** (known to Valandras)
- Annual **Phoenix Festival** — engineered civic ceremony; lanterns lit in sequence. Most recent festival failed catastrophically (Session 1)
- **Arcane substrate** — pre-Cataclysm Magi Council civic infrastructure runs beneath the streets; factions use it unseen
- **Phoenix Spire** — the city's religious and arcane institution; Boddyknock holds a chair there
- **Phoenix Heart** — mythic power source beneath Avalor; three eye-keys needed to open it; the Hollowed Ones' goal

## Elendar (Echos setting)

- Major city; the Festival of the Sealed Doors is an 8-day annual rite
- **The Diadem** — the Basilica of the Sealed Doors + Vigil garrison; arch beneath the Forum plaza is Azteroth's prison
- **Liminal Borgo** — the fringe district; where the party is currently based (the Mile-End inn)
- **The Mile-End** — Ebon Veil safe haven; Brogan's inn; carved raven mark on the doorpost

## Cross-Campaign Concepts

### The Phoenix Heart / The Arch
Two different campaign MacGuffins, but both involve ancient infrastructure and imprisoned entities:
- **CotAS:** Phoenix Heart under Avalor — requires three noble-house eye-keys
- **Echos:** Azteroth's prison arch under Elendar's Forum plaza — requires four soul-fragments + four generals

### The Whisperbond (Echos)
Illegal psychic bond technology. Keld has used it to secretly bond Crimson Storm officers (including Halios Swiftshadow). Bond-Sense: the bonded officer detects the bond-holder's bead at 60 ft. Fire-weakness is a theme in bond mechanics. Established at table in S082 via Brogan's leg story.

### Hollowed Ones (CotAS)
Criminal underground faction. Motto: "POWER IS NOTHING. CONTROL IS EVERYTHING." Doctrine of control over raw force — use mind-thralls ("the Empty"), kenku mercenaries, Illithid advisors. Symbol: the Hollow Crown (broken crown + single eye). Currently hold two of three Phoenix Heart eye-keys.

### Ebon Veil (Echos)
Underground resistance against Azteroth's release. Members: Oswald D'emon (handler), Mirael and Raine (Coffin Hunters), Brogan (innkeeper, the Mile-End). Safe-haven mark: carved raven's foot on doorposts.

### Crimson Storm / Twilight Guard (Echos)
The Crimson Storm is Il Tornja's military-religious order — the pious outer face. The Twilight Guard is the apex: four generals (Il Tornja, Keld, Vespera, Kaelum) who know the true goal is Azteroth's release. Rank-and-file know nothing; they believe they're serving Pyrian.
